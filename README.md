# TappyTimmy
## Controls:
/* Jump = Space Bar 
Dash = Right Arrow key
Restart = 'r' key

## Goal:
Last as long as you can without running into any obstacles.


## How it was similar or differed from original One-Pager:
1. Original Concept and Inspiration
Starting from my original one-pager, I used *Geometry Dash* as the foundation for Tappy Timmy. However, as I developed the game, I also incorporated mechanics similar to the Chrome Dinosaur game that appears when a browser has no internet connection.One of the biggest changes I made was deciding to create a continuous game rather than having a specific endpoint. This allowed me to implement a score counter that tracks how long the player survives, giving players a reason to keep playing and improve their previous scores.

2. Gameplay Mechanics and Difficulty
I wanted the game to remain challenging even though it had no endpoint. To accomplish this, I programmed the obstacles to spawn at random and gradually increased how frequently they appeared.I also made the overall game speed increase over time, causing the obstacles and scrolling ground to move faster. This creates a progressively more difficult experience as the player survives longer.

3. Audio Implementation
One challenge I encountered was incorporating audio into the game. I initially struggled because I didn't know how to create my own sound effects or where to find free audio resources.After exploring different options, I found sound effects through the Unity Asset Store and background music through Adobe Stock. I used these resources to add more personality to the game and make the gameplay more engaging.

4. Design Changes and Limitations
In my original concept, I planned to incorporate power-ups. However, as I worked on the randomized obstacle spawning system, I realized that adding power-ups would make the game more complicated to develop.Instead, I decided to focus on the main gameplay mechanics and keep the controls simple. This helped me prioritize making the game functional and enjoyable rather than adding features I wasn't confident I could fully implement. For the background, I kept the design relatively simple. I added clouds that spawn randomly at different times, speeds, and sizes. This gives the background some movement and variety without distracting players from the obstacles.

5. Final Reflection
Overall, Tappy Timmy changed from my original one-pager as I became more familiar with Unity and the process of developing a game. I learned that some ideas were more difficult to implement than I originally expected, which required me to adjust my plans and prioritize certain features.Although I wasn't able to include everything from my initial concept, I was able to create a continuous game with increasing difficulty, randomized obstacles, a scoring system, sound effects, and a moving background. This project helped me better understand how different game mechanics work together and how important it is to balance creative ideas with what is realistically achievable.
